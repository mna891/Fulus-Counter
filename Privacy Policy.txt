4. الاتصال بالإنترنت والطرف الثالث:
- التطبيق يعمل بالكامل دون الحاجة إلى اتصال بالإنترنت (Offline-first).
- التطبيق خالٍ من أي مكتبات إعلانية أو أدوات تتبع وتحليل سلوكي (No Ads, No Third-party Trackers).

[EN] English

This Privacy Policy explains how "Fulus Counter" handles your data and privacy.

1. Data Collection and Usage:
- No Personal Data Collected: The app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information (e.g., name, email, phone number, device identifiers).
- No Account Needed: The app requires no registration or account creation.
- Local Storage Only: All currency calculations, denominations, cash reconciliation counts, history logs, and exchange rate preferences are stored and processed strictly on your local device. No data is ever transmitted to external servers or third parties.

2. Device Permissions:
- VIBRATE (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used solely to provide tactile feedback (Haptic Feedback) when tapping counter buttons and adding bundles, simulating physical cash counter machines.

3. Sharing and Exporting Receipts:
- When exporting calculation reports or sharing receipt images, the image is rendered locally in the application cache and transferred directly to the user-selected application via Android standard sharing intents.

4. Offline Capability and Third-Party Services:
- The app operates entirely offline.
- No third-party analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs are embedded in the application.
